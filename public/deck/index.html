The problem with AI triage

“Confirmed” is not evidence.

A model that reads a bug report and says "confirmed" agrees with whatever the report claims.

Allowance · v0.1.0allowance.edycu.dev
Most AI issue bots read the report and summarise it back. That is agreement, not evidence. We wanted a verdict a maintainer can check.
